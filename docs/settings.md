# User Settings

---

## Slack

Link your slack account to receive schedule reminders and updates! 
1. Click the 📋 icon next to the pink text to copy the link command.
2. Click the now enabled "Open Slack" button.
3. Paste the command into any channel in your robotics slack.
4. Your account is linked! The Slack Account section should now be updated.

## Reset Password

To reset your password, create a new password and enter it twice.
Resetting your password will log you out on all devices.
Note that for account recovery, admins can reset user password.

## Remove All Sessions

This will logout your account on all devices, including the one you are currently on.

## Logout

This will logout your account on the device you are currently on.
