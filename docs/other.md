# Other Pages

## Scout Lead

This page is only available to users with the LEAD or ADMIN permissions.

The Lead Page lets you set the current match number. Clicking "Set Match" will also assign currently available scouts to scout the match.

The table shows scouts that were assigned teams to scout last match.

## Pit Monitor

Sections / Tabs:

- Upcoming Matches
    - Uses Nexus data to give estimated time and team information for your teams upcoming matches.
- Nexus
    - Shows which match is On Deck, Queuing, and Queuing Soon.
- In Pit Now
    - Shows which users are assigned to Pit currently and how long they will be there.
- Team Ranking
    - Shows WLT, RP, and EPA data for your team from various sources.
- Battery Data
    - Shows the current battery that is in the robot and the one that has been charging the longest.

## Shift Tracker

This page lets you to see who is currently on a specific assignment, how long they have left, and users who will be on the assignment soon (in the next block).

## Battery Manager

This page allows you to keep track of which batteries have been charging the longest, which are inactive, and which are in the robot. It also displays some of this data on the pit monitor.
